Support for your mental health

Free and confidential ways to get help. Most of these are available whenever you need them, day or night, and none of them require you to say who you are if you don't want to.

If it's urgent

  • 999if there's immediate danger to life
  • Samaritanscall 116 123 — free, confidential, any age, 24/7
  • SHOUTtext SHOUT to 85258 — free 24/7 crisis text support
  • Childlinecall 0800 1111 or chat online — for under 19s, 24/7
  • NHS 111call 111 and choose the mental health option
  • HOPELINE247 (Papyrus)call 0300 102 2470 or text 07786 209697 — for under 35s having thoughts of suicide

Talk to someone

  • The Mixcall 0808 808 4994 (11am–11pm) or webchat — for under 25s
  • Koothfree, anonymous online counselling and peer support
  • YoungMindsadvice and self-help guides, plus a parents' helpline
  • CALMcall 0800 58 58 58 (5pm–midnight) or webchat
  • FRANKcall 0300 123 6600 or text 82111 — for drugs and alcohol
  • Beatcall 0808 801 0677 (England) or 0808 801 0433 (Wales) — for eating disorders
  • Mermaidscall 0808 801 0400 or webchat — for trans, non-binary and gender-diverse young people
  • Muslim Youth Helplinecall 0808 808 2008
  • Switchboard LGBT+call 0800 0119 100 or webchat

Apps worth trying

  • NHS Every Mind Matterspractical tips on sleep, stress and social media
  • Calm Harmtechniques to help manage urges to self-harm
  • BlueIcemood diary and safety planning tools
  • Stay Alivesuicide prevention and safety planning
  • SAMfor anxiety, low mood and everyday stress
  • Feeling Good Teensaudio tracks for relaxation, sleep and exam stress

Find something local

  • Hub of Hopea searchable directory of UK mental health services
  • Youth Accessfind free local counselling and advice services near you
